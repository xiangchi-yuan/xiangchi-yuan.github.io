Research Summary and Impact

My research interests include agentic reasoning, SFT/RL post-training, and long-context foundation models. I am especially interested in making reasoning systems more capable, efficient, and reliable.

RAM in K2-Horizon’s flagship post-training

The Institute of Foundation Models (IFM) applied our Reinforced Agent Merging (RAM) to K2-Horizon-375B-A23B, which ranked in the top 10 among <500B open source models on representative Artificial Analysis measures (#5 Terminal-Bench 2.1; #10 Intelligence Index ≈ GPT-6 Luna (Medium), as of November 2026). Instead of using mixed RL or on-policy distillation (OPD) to consolidate capabilities, K2-Horizon applied RAM as a core component of post-training consolidation, combining five separately trained RL specialists into a single checkpoint. After consolidation, the model outperformed the corresponding specialists on BrowseComp (62.5% → 68.1%), IFBench (60.7% → 65.2%), and Omniscience (23.8% → 24.5%) and improved over the starting checkpoint on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (37.8% → 46.1%) without a dedicated terminal specialist. RAM was also applied to Uno, the best-performing diffusion LLM on the reported agentic coding and long-context reasoning benchmarks as of Sep 2026.
