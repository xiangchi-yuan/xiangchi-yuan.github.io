Xiangchi Yuan

Hi! I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Wenke Lee.

I work on training large foundation models to reason and solve complex tasks, with a focus on agentic systems, reinforcement learning, and long-context modeling. I am fortunate to collaborate with wonderful researchers across academia and industry.

Previously, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Brandeis University and my B.Eng. from UESTC and the University of Glasgow.

Research

My research interests include agentic reasoning, SFT/RL post-training, and long-context foundation models. I am especially interested in making reasoning systems more capable, efficient, and reliable.
